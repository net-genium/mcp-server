## How to use the examples

- **Write about your company, not about software.** The descriptions that work best explain how the process runs at your company: who creates what, what is recorded and which statuses a record goes through. You do not have to name tables or data types — the AI proposes them.
- **The generator** asks for two things: the name of the application and a description. Put the name from the example into the field “Enter the application name”, the description into “Enter a description of your new application”, press “Create application” and answer whether you want sample data as well — with it you can click through the finished application straight away. The generator builds the forms, the relations between them and the sums, plus one view page with a list of records for each main form; filtered overviews, buttons with scripts and boards are added by the agent.
- **The agent** gets the prompt in the conversation. Before writing anything it shows you what it is about to create or change and you approve the proposal; each example therefore says what exists once you agree.
- Rewrite the names of statuses, categories and roles to match your own — the AI takes them literally. The actual result may differ slightly from the description below, because the AI proposes the structure; the sentences “What is created” describe what came out in our verification.
